My Journey

Hello! I am Anish Kumar, a tech enthusiast and web developer currently pursuing my Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering at Government Polytechnic Gaya, Bihar (2025–2028). Prior to this, I completed my schooling at Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Sitamarhi with 84% in CBSE boards.

My passion for software and web development started with curiosity about how the web works and quickly grew into building practical web applications. Whether it's coding procedural logic in Python or architecting responsive user interfaces with HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript, I love turning concepts into tangible interactive software.
